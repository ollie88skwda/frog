The idea, realized

The grid is the surface, not decoration. Conditions are fill-in-the-blank lines, the set log is a ruled table on the paper, notes are margin // comments. It reads as a notebook the instant you open it.

Light vs. dark tradeoff

Light graph paper is gorgeous and the most distinctive — but check it under harsh gym lights / outdoors (glare, battery). The navy blueprint keeps the exact same notebook feel while staying easy to read mid-set.

Open question for you

Want one identity (pick light or blueprint), or ship both as a theme toggle — paper by day, blueprint in the gym? Easy to support since it's all CSS variables.

Legibility guardrails

Grid stays faint; ink stays high-contrast (graphite on warm white, near-white on navy) so text clears WCAG AA. The grid never sits directly behind a number's value.
