Frog · Gym Tracker

Design Direction — Same Screen, Three Ways

Each phone shows the active-workout logging screen (the heart of the app): an exercise with joint-action tags, the saved machine setup, last-session ghost values, a progression nudge, an expanded set with RIR + a custom metric, and a running rest timer. The screen is identical in content — only the design language changes.

My pick: Direction 1 — Quiet Instrument. The design intelligence backs it: a Dark-Mode (OLED) system scores Excellent performance + WCAG AAA and is rated best for low-light, glanceable, data-heavy use — exactly a phone held mid-set under harsh gym lighting. Tabular monospaced numerals keep weight/reps/RIR columns rock-steady, and a single orange accent means the one thing that matters (your next action) is the only thing that glows. Direction 3 is the strong runner-up for energy; Direction 2 is beautiful but light mode fights gym readability.

The decision, scored

Across the five things that matter for a phone used mid-set — glanceability, gym-lighting legibility, data density, power-user feel, distinctiveness — Direction 1 wins on the first four. Dark OLED + tabular mono is purpose-built for "read a number in half a second."

Recommended tokens (Dir. 1)

BG #0B0F17 · Surface #131A26 · Line rgba(255,255,255,.08) · Text #F4F7FB · Muted #8A97AB · Accent #F97316 · Good #34D399.

Type pairing

UI: Inter (400–700). Numbers: JetBrains Mono with tabular figures so columns never shift. Both ship free and load fast in Expo.

Note

These are static comparison mockups (one screen). They show look & feel, not final spacing or every state. Once you pick, the winner's tokens go into the spec as the design system.
