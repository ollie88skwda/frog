FR G

A training lab notebook that a frog wandered into and now runs.

Serious lifters log the work — reps × weight, RIR — and the conditions around it: sleep, bodyweight, carbs, caffeine, stress. Frog surfaces honest correlations with visible confidence and an unbroken habit of reminding you it is only a correlation. The statistics are exact. The frog is an idiot. Both, deliberately, at once.

North star

The driest, most rigorous instrument you’ve ever trusted — and, unmistakably, a frog runs it.

If a lifter ever distrusts a Finding because the app “seems like a joke,” the balance is wrong. Everything below exists to keep the numbers credible while the frog gets to be a goofball at the edges.

01

The split — one principle

The whole brand rides on one rule. Print it on the wall.

The closer to the data, the more serious. The closer to the edges, the more the frog is an idiot.

◻ Sacred — never goofy

  • The logging hot path (set → save, <100ms)
  • Charts, e1RM, timers, tabular numbers
  • Findings: the statistic, its confidence, its caveat
  • Anything a lifter makes a training decision on

Quiet · precise · tabular · brutalist. The frog does not clutter the data.

◆ Playground — the frog lives here

  • Wordmark & mascot, onboarding, empty states
  • Loading, PR celebrations, streaks, rest-timer alerts
  • Errors, 404s, notifications, achievement names
  • The settings / about corners

Deadpan · dry · quietly absurd. Never random. Never cartoonish.

The comedy is the contrast, delivered straight-faced next to real numbers — not the app being random.

Semantic — kept legible, separate from accent

✓ success = frog green ! warning = amber ✕ error = red

Success is the frog — a PR, a green trend, a completed session all mean “good / go / alive.” Amber = caution / low confidence. Red = regression / error. Only ever three signal colors; no blue competes for attention.

Discipline held: single base color, percentage steps — same rule you have today. We swapped the base hue from blue to neutral ink and moved the one accent to green. Nothing else multiplies.

04

Logomark — four directions

Each must survive at 16px and feel engineered even while being a frog. No cute blobs. Shown large, then at favicon size.

16px

A · Grid Frog

Built rectilinear on the 8px grid — two eye-blocks, a wide mouth, green pupils. Reads as pure engineering. The most brutalist option; the frog is implied by structure, not softness.

16px

B · Frog-Eye = Data Point

A single hooded frog eye whose pupil is a plotted data point sitting on a crosshair — and doubles as a decimal point. The whole thesis in one mark: an eye watching your data. Cleanest at 16px (a ringed green dot).

16px

C · Frog on Graph Paper

A single-line sitting frog perched on a graph baseline — the lab-notebook soul made literal. Warmest, most narrative option. Loses a little detail at 16px but the silhouette holds.

16px

D · Single-Line Frog

One continuous stroke: two eye-humps and a wide flat grin. Ultra-minimal, works as an inline glyph, animates as a single path. The friendliest; use for the mascot’s lightweight “face” cue.

Decision · locked: B (Frog-Eye = Data Point) is the primary mark — chrome, favicon, app icon. It carries the full “watching your data” thesis and is bulletproof at 16px. D is the mascot’s minimal face cue; C is for playground/marketing warmth; A is the monochrome stamp fallback. The wordmark uses B in place of the “O”.
05

Typography

Keep the pairing you have — evolve its discipline. Numbers are the serious anchor and are always mono & tabular. That rule is non-negotiable; it is what makes the data look measured.

Display · headings
Frog

Bricolage Grotesque, 700–800, tight tracking (−0.03em). Carries warmth & personality — this is where headings are allowed to smile.

Body · prose

Log the work, and the conditions around it.

Inter / system sans, 400–500. Quiet, neutral, high-legibility. Never competes with the data.

Data · the anchor
142.5kg

JetBrains Mono / Commit Mono, tabular-nums. Every weight, rep, RIR, e1RM, timer, p-value. Digits line up in columns, always.

Tabular proof — why mono numbers matter
Squat142.5 kge1RM 168.2
Bench100.0 kge1RM 118.9
Deadlift180.0 kge1RM 205.7

Columns align to the decimal because the figures are monospaced. Proportional numerals would ripple — and rippling numbers read as “approximate.” Frog’s numbers are never approximate.

Fit with the shipped system: Bricolage is already wired as Radix’s --default-font-family; numeric data already runs through a num utility (system mono + tabular-nums) because Bricolage has no tabular figures. A branded mono (JetBrains / Commit Mono) is optional — self-host and set --font-mono. Nothing here changes the type stack; it endorses it. (This page falls back to a grotesque + system mono; font CDNs are blocked in artifacts.)

Look & feel

How “goofy frog + serious lab notebook + brutalist terminal” coheres: graph-paper surfaces, deadpan line-art, terminal-green cues, sparse ripple motifs. Flat, 0px radius, 1px borders — nothing slows the data path.

Sacred proof · a real Findings card
◻ finding7d sleep vs top set
+4.2% heavier

on nights with 7h+ sleep · n=19 sessions

confidence95%

correlation, not causation. controls: bodyweight, caffeine.

Zero jokes. Faint grid, area fill, emphasized endpoint, tabular figures, an honest confidence bar and caveat. This is the instrument.

Motifs
  • Graph paper — faint 8px/24px grid on empty surfaces & playground zones. The notebook you scribble in.
  • Dissection-diagram line art — deadpan labelled illustrations (a frog, a barbell) with leader lines & specimen numbers.
  • Terminal-green cues — the accent as a phosphor blink: cursor, active state, the one thing that’s alive.
  • Lily-pad ripple — a single expanding ring for a “leap” moment. Sparse. Never on the data.
Surfaces
Flat — no shadows 0px radius 1px hairline borders 8px grid spacing Dense 13px UI Dark default + light

Buttons always carry a visible background — never bare text. The frog is loud; the surfaces are silent.

08

Motion

Micro-interactions 100–150ms. Nothing animates on the data path. A restrained “leap” or “ripple” earns a smile at the edges — never under a number a lifter is reading.

Allowed · playground

A single lily-pad ripple on a PR banner. A 120ms “leap” (translateY) on a streak. A confidence bar that fills once on Findings reveal. Cursor/phosphor blink on active input.

Forbidden · sacred

No animation on set-save, on the logging keypad, on live charts, on timers, or on any figure. Feedback there is instant state change (<100ms), not motion.

Findings reveal

The statistic appears immediately; only its confidence bar animates its width once (900ms ease-out) as it scrolls in — the reveal is a flourish on the frame, never on the number.

demo88%

All motion respects prefers-reduced-motion — ripples and fills disable cleanly, state still reads.

09

Mascot usage — where the frog may & may not appear

The single most important guardrail. The expressive mascot lives only in the playground. In the sacred zones, at most the abstract mark (B) may appear as quiet chrome — never the character.

May appear

  • ✓ Wordmark, app icon, splash, onboarding
  • ✓ Empty states & “not enough data yet”
  • ✓ Loading screens
  • ✓ PR celebrations, streaks, achievement names
  • ✓ Rest-timer alerts (the toast, not the timer face)
  • ✓ Errors, failed-sync, 404s
  • ✓ Notifications & push copy
  • ✓ Settings / about / changelog corners

May NOT appear

  • ✕ The logging hot path (set entry → save)
  • ✕ Charts, graphs, sparklines
  • ✕ Numbers, e1RM, RIR, bodyweight fields
  • ✕ The running timer face
  • ✕ A Finding’s statistic, confidence, or caveat
  • ✕ Anything used to make a training decision
  • ✕ Export / API / data-table views
  • ✕ Never as a “helpful assistant” explaining the math

Test before shipping any frog: “Is a lifter about to make a decision from this pixel?” If yes, the frog leaves the room.

Frog is a re-skin of the shipped Radix Themes 3 system, not a rebuild. Every decision above is one prop or one token swap. Here is the exact diff and, for each, whether Radix supports it natively.

All six are native Radix Theme props. * radius="none" restores the brief’s 0px mandate — a deliberate reversal, decided below.

theme.css — the scale imports

- @import ".../colors/indigo.css"; /* accent */ - @import ".../colors/slate.css"; /* gray */ + @import ".../colors/grass.css"; /* accent */ + @import ".../colors/sage.css"; /* gray */ @import ".../colors/red.css"; /* --neg */ @import ".../colors/amber.css"; /* --warn */ @import ".../colors/orange.css"; /* --orange*/ /* success folds into accent → drop green.css */ /* bridge remaps (locked scales, no light/dark dupes) */ --accent: var(--grass-9); --brand: var(--grass-9); --ring: var(--grass-8); --pos: var(--grass-11); /* success = frog */ --border: var(--sage-6); --ink: var(--sage-12);

Honors the file’s own 6-scale budget — this ends at 5 scales (sage · grass · red · amber · orange), one lighter than today. Referencing an un-imported scale silently breaks, so the swap is import-for-import.

Decisions · locked
  1. Radius → none (0px). Deliberate reversal of the recent radius="small" call. Brutalist 0px is load-bearing for the terminal/lab-notebook soul — sharp corners read as “precise instrument.” Fallback if mobile tap-feel testing regresses: radius="small" on interactive controls only.
  2. Accent → built-in grass for v1. Zero custom CSS, accessible, and it keeps the bundle light. Reads a shade more leaf-green than the #43DD6E neon; a generated custom scale can hit the exact neon later without touching anything else.
  3. Success = the frog. Semantic success folds into the accent green (a PR is the frog’s green). Drops the separate green scale → 5 scales total, and reinforces the brand.
