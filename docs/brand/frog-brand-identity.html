01

The split — one principle

The whole brand rides on one rule. Print it on the wall.

The closer to the data, the more serious. The closer to the edges, the more the frog is an idiot.

◻ Sacred — never goofy

  • The logging hot path (set → save, <100ms)
  • Charts, e1RM, timers, tabular numbers
  • Findings: the statistic, its confidence, its caveat
  • Anything a lifter makes a training decision on

Quiet · precise · tabular · brutalist.

◆ Playground — the frog lives here

  • Wordmark & mascot, onboarding, empty states
  • Loading, PR celebrations, streaks, alerts
  • Errors, 404s, notifications, achievement names
  • The settings / about corners

Deadpan · dry · quietly absurd. Never random.

The frog

One character. A serious mark for the chrome; an expressive mascot for the playground. Both, so neither compromises.

Persona · 3 lines

Dr. Frog, PhD (unaccredited). An amphibian who read every paper on autoregulation and now runs a lab out of a pond.

Deadpan, exacting, and quietly obsessed with your sleep data — and with whether you actually hit RIR 1.

Will present a p-value with a completely straight face, then remind you it is only a correlation.

The mark

A literal frog, seen head-on, sitting on a ground line: broad rounded body, a head that bulges into a hump over each eye, two big dark eyes with a light glint, two nostrils, one long flat mouth, two front feet, two haunches. It is a trace of the supplied art, kept in docs/brand/frog-source-1024.png — centrelines sampled off that bitmap and fitted, not redrawn by eye (a redraw drifted once already). apps/web/public/icon.svg is the single vector source and every PNG is a render of it (bun scripts/gen-pwa-icons.ts); never hand-edit a PNG, and re-fit against the reference rather than nudging control points.

16px

Standalone tile

Browser favicon, PWA icon, iOS home screen. Ships on the source art’s own grass green #6AB347 with the frog as black line art — outline, feet, mouth and ground bar in #131426, both values sampled straight out of the reference bitmap. The body carries the same green as the ground, so only the outline separates them; the eye glints are the one light note (sage #ECEEED) against the dark eyes. Ground and line work move together — flipping one alone breaks the mark. Two stroke weights in the mark’s 572 × 355.5 art space: 26 for the haunches, body, feet and ground bar, 19 for the mouth; the small-size treatment takes them to 36/26. The manifest stays on lab ink #101211 for background_color/theme_color: those paint the PWA splash and the browser chrome, which belong to the app shell, not the tile. 0px radius: iOS rounds its own corners, so never bake one in. What the tile shows is the icon cut, not the whole mark — see below.

In-app mark

Sidebar and sign-in, at 20–32px. Same silhouette, simplified: no haunches, nostrils or eye glints — they turn to mud at that size. Outline is currentColor and the body var(--accent), never a fixed hex, so it survives both themes; a fixed dark outline vanishes on the dark sage surface. This is the one place the mark is not the tile’s fixed black-on-green — the tile palette is fixed, the app chrome follows the theme. Strokes run ~1.35× the scaled weight (1.3/0.95) so they clear a pixel; push them to 1.75 and the eye humps close over the eyes. Component: components/frog-mark.tsx.

The tiles ship an icon cut, not the whole mark. The mark is 1.6:1, so it cannot fill a square: centred and sized to the width it leaves top/bottom margins ~2.5× the sides and reads as a frog floating in a mostly-empty tile — which is exactly how it shipped, and how it looked on a real home screen. An app-icon crop is routinely tighter than the full brand mark, so the tiles use a 469-unit square window on the head + body, bottom-anchored: the body fills 93% of the width, the haunches, feet and ground bar run off the left and right edges (a bleeding ground line reads deliberate; a floating one reads like a mistake), and the bar sits 3.5% above the bottom edge so the frog sits on the tile floor with the headroom above it. That is the window icon.svg itself carries, so the favicon and the manifest’s SVG icon match the PNG tiles. Nothing is distorted — the cut preserves the mark’s aspect ratio, it just shows less of it. apple-touch-icon is exactly 180 × 180 and fully opaque (iOS discards alpha and composites on black) with no baked corner radius. maskable is the exception and gets the whole mark: platforms crop it to an arbitrary shape and only the inner-80% circle is guaranteed, so nothing may bleed. It is scaled until its farthest painted point lands on that circle — 70% of the tile, with the mark lifted 3% so the ground bar’s end caps stop wasting the circle. scripts/gen-pwa-icons.ts derives both windows from the mark’s measured geometry, so the cut follows the frog rather than being maintained beside it.

Below 25px the outline falls under a pixel and the fine detail hazes over, so icon.svg carries a size media query: at 16–24px it drops the nostrils and glints and thickens every stroke. The eyes need more than that — they are dark and sit under a brow that is also dark, so a thickened brow simply swallows them and the frog loses its face. At that size the eye collapses to its glint: light, smaller, dropped clear of the brow. That is the small sample above — not a muddy downscale.

03

Language registers — Human · Frog · Ultrafrog

One setting changes how the whole app talks — never what it reports. The numbers are identical in every register; only the words around them change. Flip the switch below (or in the top bar) and watch this entire page re-voice itself.

Live — affects every sample of app copy on this page.

Human

Plain, clear, zero personality. The accessibility / serious-mode escape hatch. What a lifter switches to when they want facts and nothing else.

Rest complete — 3:00 elapsed. Next: bench 100.0 kg.

Frog · default

The deadpan brand voice. Dry, exacting, too into frogs. Editorialises around the number, never on it.

Rest complete. The frog suggests you pick up the bar in 3:00. Next: bench 100.0 kg.

Ultrafrog

Every word becomes ribbit (case preserved). Numbers and units survive untouched — so all information rides on the figures. Absurd, and a live proof that the data is the only thing that matters.

Ribbit ribbit. Ribbit ribbit ribbit ribbit ribbit ribbit 3:00. Ribbit: ribbit 100.0 kg.
The rule that makes Ultrafrog safe: the transform keeps any token containing a digit, plus a whitelist of units (kg, lb, %, s, RIR, e1RM, n, ×). Everything a lifter needs to make a decision survives; everything else ribbits. This is the split principle, executed in grammar.

Engineering note: registers are a pure text transform at render time. Data, timers, and charts are untouched — Ultrafrog is a skin on strings, never on state. Human is also the i18n seam: real translations slot in beside the frog voice.

Do / don’t

✓ Do

  • ✓ State the real number first or last; let the frog editorialise in between.
  • ✓ Deadpan. Understate. The frog is impressed by nodding slowly.
  • ✓ Use real terms: e1RM, RIR, correlation, n=, p-value.
  • ✓ Keep the caveat: “correlation, not causation.”
  • ✓ Sentence case. Periods. One idea per line.

✕ Don’t

  • ✕ Let a joke touch the figure itself.
  • ✕ Exclaim. No “!”, no “Oops!”, no “Woohoo!”.
  • ✕ Emoji inside data, charts, or Findings.
  • ✕ Be zany or random. The humour is contrast, not chaos.
  • ✕ Fake certainty — never round a caveat away.

09

Numbers & data — the sacred spec

This is the instrument. No jokes, no mascot, no register change touches it. Consistency here is what lets the frog be an idiot everywhere else.

Sacred proof · Findings card
◻ finding7d sleep vs top set
+4.2% heavier

on nights with 7h+ sleep · n=19 sessions

confidence95%

correlation, not causation. controls: bodyweight, caffeine.

Faint grid, area fill, emphasised endpoint, tabular figures, honest confidence bar + mandatory caveat.

Rules for every number
  • Mono & tabular always — weights, reps, RIR, e1RM, timers, p-values.
  • Decimals aligned; fixed dp per type (weight 1, correlation 2, p 3).
  • Units after a hair space: 172.4 kg, not 172.4kg.
  • Time as m:ss (3:00), never 180s in the UI.
  • Confidence is visible — a bar + %, never hidden.
  • Caveat is mandatory — “correlation, not causation” ships with every Finding.
  • No black boxes — formulas (e1RM) are inspectable; the frog shows its working.
  • Never round a lie — n is shown; low n reads “not enough data.”

11

Motion

Micro-interactions 100–150ms. Nothing animates on the data path. A restrained “leap” or “ripple” earns a smile at the edges — never under a number.

Allowed · playground

A lily-pad ripple on a PR banner. A 120ms leap on a streak. A confidence bar that fills once. Cursor blink on active input.

Forbidden · sacred

No animation on set-save, keypad, live charts, timers, or any figure. Feedback there is instant state change (<100ms), not motion.

Findings reveal

The statistic appears immediately; only its confidence bar animates once (900ms) on scroll-in — a flourish on the frame, never the number.

demo88%

All motion respects prefers-reduced-motion — ripples and fills disable cleanly, state still reads.

12

Accessibility

A rigorous instrument is legible to everyone. The frog never gets in the way of a lifter reading their numbers.

Hard requirements
  • Contrast — body text ≥ 4.5:1, large ≥ 3:1, on both themes (Radix step 11/12 guarantees it).
  • Focus visible — 2px accent ring, 2px offset, on every interactive element.
  • Tap targets — ≥ 44px on the logging path.
  • 16px inputs — never smaller (blocks iOS zoom).
  • Never color alone — semantics carry a glyph + word too.
  • Reduced motion — honored; nothing essential lives in animation.
Humour never blocks clarity

Human register is an accessibility feature. A lifter who finds the frog tiring — or who relies on a screen reader and wants plain strings — flips to Human and gets clean, literal copy with the same numbers. Ultrafrog is opt-in and never the default. The frog is a personality, not a barrier.

Human = plain-language mode
13

Mascot usage — where the frog may & may not appear

The single most important guardrail. The expressive mascot lives only in the playground. In sacred zones, at most the mark appears as quiet chrome — never the character.

May appear

  • ✓ Wordmark, app icon, splash, onboarding
  • ✓ Empty states & “not enough data yet”
  • ✓ Loading screens
  • ✓ PR celebrations, streaks, achievement names
  • ✓ Rest-timer alerts (the toast, not the timer face)
  • ✓ Errors, failed-sync, 404s
  • ✓ Notifications & push copy
  • ✓ Settings / about / changelog corners

May NOT appear

  • ✕ The logging hot path (set entry → save)
  • ✕ Charts, graphs, sparklines
  • ✕ Numbers, e1RM, RIR, bodyweight fields
  • ✕ The running timer face
  • ✕ A Finding’s statistic, confidence, or caveat
  • ✕ Anything used to make a training decision
  • ✕ Export / API / data-table views
  • ✕ Never as a “helpful assistant” explaining the math

Test before shipping any frog: “Is a lifter about to make a decision from this pixel?” If yes, the frog leaves the room.

theme.css — the scale imports

- @import ".../colors/indigo.css"; /* accent */ - @import ".../colors/slate.css"; /* gray */ + @import ".../colors/grass.css"; /* accent */ + @import ".../colors/sage.css"; /* gray */ @import ".../colors/red.css"; /* --neg */ @import ".../colors/amber.css"; /* --warn */ @import ".../colors/orange.css"; /* --orange*/ /* success folds into accent → drop green.css */ /* bridge remaps (locked scales, no light/dark dupes) */ --accent: var(--grass-9); --brand: var(--grass-9); --ring: var(--grass-8); --pos: var(--grass-11); /* success = frog */ --border: var(--sage-6); --ink: var(--sage-12);

Honors the file’s 6-scale budget — ends at 5 scales (sage · grass · red · amber · orange), one lighter than today. Referencing an un-imported scale silently breaks, so the swap is import-for-import.

Decisions · locked
  1. Radius → none (0px). Deliberate reversal of the recent radius="small" call. Brutalist 0px is load-bearing for the terminal/lab soul. Fallback if mobile tap-feel regresses: small on interactive controls only.
  2. Accent → built-in grass for v1. Zero custom CSS, accessible, keeps the bundle light. Custom neon scale later without touching anything else.
  3. Success = the frog. Folds into the accent green → 5 scales total, reinforces the brand.
